Reading the landscape

A parish entry, a census-night visit and a long-term home tell different stories. The labels on this page preserve those distinctions. A date is a dated piece of evidence, not necessarily the beginning or end of a tenancy.

Street names, numbering and buildings change. King William Street near Trafalgar Square is now William IV Street; the original Adelphi riverfront terrace was demolished. Approximate markers help locate the neighbourhood without presenting a modern building as a verified ancestral house.
