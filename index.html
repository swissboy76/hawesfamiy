II.

The surgeon of the Strand

William Henry Bartleman Hawes (1812–1857) is listed as a surgeon at 406 Strand in an 1852 directory and as a chemist on the Strand in an 1854 bankruptcy notice. His qualifications and any Elizabeth Fry connection remain unverified.

05 / Open research

Questions still to answer

These are research tasks, not conclusions. Resolving them will allow the biography to develop into a documented history.

What did William Edward do?

Inspect the occupation columns of the 1871, 1881 and 1891 census returns. The family remembers banking, but a bank and role have not been established.

Where was the Thornton Heath home?

Transcribe the address on the 1881 census page RG11/820, folio 88, page 31, and check later returns.

Which Sophia was William Henry's wife?

The GEDCOM contains Sophia Maria Davison and Sophia Maria Davison Day in separate family units. Inspect the 1843 marriage and 1844 baptism entries before merging them.

Was there a Charing Cross or Fry link?

Look for staff or student records at Charing Cross Hospital and contemporary correspondence relating to Elizabeth Fry. No direct link is established.

What was the history of 243 and 406 Strand?

406 Strand appears in a published 1852 directory. The 243 Strand address is a family lead and remains independently unconfirmed.

What was Walter's clerical career?

Family information describes Walter Hawes (born c. 1879) as a clerk. The employer, industry and precise job title still require a census or directory entry.
