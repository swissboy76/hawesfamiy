New census evidence

The Hawes household in Lambeth, 1891

A supplied census transcript records Sophia Maira D. Hawes, Mary Emma, Caroline Maria and a four-year-old grandson named Walter J. Wm. Hawes. This Walter is not assumed to be Walter Hawes born around 1879. The new evidence is recorded separately until we can inspect the original page and confirm the relationships.

A shared family project

Not one person’s ancestry. Our shared story.

The archive is organised by people and family relationships rather than by one descendant’s perspective. The current GEDCOM covers several historical Hawes branches, including the children of William and Elizabeth Mullinex Hawes, the large family of Thomas Henry Hawes and the descendants of William Henry Bartleman Hawes. The family tree will grow as more relatives contribute photographs, documentary evidence and memories.

Privacy and accuracy: Details of living family members are not published from the GEDCOM. A relationship in an Ancestry tree is not proof by itself. Historical portraits are only displayed when tied to an identifiable source, and unverified family recollections are labelled.
