The research standard

Evidence comes first

Family memories and online trees are invaluable clues, but we do not present them as archival proof. Unverified details are labelled and portraits are never invented.

01

Records

We prioritise census returns, parish registers, contemporary directories, wills and dated newspaper reports.

02

Family knowledge

Recollections about banking, clerical work and family residences remain visible as research leads.

03

Images

Real portraits can be added as they are identified. A neutral placeholder appears where no authenticated image is available.
