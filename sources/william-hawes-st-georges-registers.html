Two family-held parish-register extracts · Westminster

William Hawes at St George's, Hanover Square

Two original register extracts provided by the family document William Hawes's birth and baptism in 1785 and his marriage to Elizabeth Mullinex in 1805. These provide direct evidence of the family's connection to St George's parish before the later records associated with the Strand and Adelphi.

Birth-date conflict: A previously cited family research date was 21 June 1785. This extract appears to specify 24 June 1785. The difference is flagged, rather than silently suppressing the earlier claim; checking the underlying 1785 register page will further confirm the handwriting.

What these records establish

The birth extract expressly states that William was born in the parish of St George, Hanover Square, and names his parents John and Ann. The marriage extract states that both William and Elizabeth were of the parish in March 1805. These are substantial documentary anchors for the Hawes family's Westminster origins. They do not, by themselves, identify the street where either person lived or establish the precise date of the subsequent move to Adelphi Terrace.

Catalogue and provenance

Two photographs of family-held St George's, Hanover Square register extracts, provided 6 November 2026. The dates above are transcribed directly from those photographs, with uncertain handwriting identified. Original register entries should be cross-referenced through the City of Westminster Archives Centre.
