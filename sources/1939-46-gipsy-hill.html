Occupation

“Store Keeper” is legible. Following words appear to begin “Head Ware…” but are cropped at the right edge. “Head warehouseman” is a possible expansion, not a verified transcription, and is not entered as an established occupation.

Research implications

Eric’s exact birthday agrees with the existing family date, and his single status precedes the documented marriage registration in April–June 1942. George’s apparent late-1910 birth date is compatible with a first-quarter 1911 registration. The Mary entry’s 17 February 1919 date is consistent with the date previously noted on Walter’s pension card, but the household identity and original wording should be checked before upgrading that earlier lead to a fully established birthday. Ellen F.’s name and date need reconciliation with the Frances Ellen Creese records.

The 1939 Register records dates as reported at the time; it is documentary support rather than a birth certificate. The partial occupation and ambiguous names are preserved as uncertain readings.
