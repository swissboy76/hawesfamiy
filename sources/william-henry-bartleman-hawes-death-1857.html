What this entry establishes

The source extract displays “Death Date: Dec 1857”. With only a quarterly index entry, the supported date is the October–December registration quarter, not a confirmed December death. A death may also precede its registration. Holborn is the registration district and does not establish an exact place of death, residence or burial.

The certificate reference is 1857, December quarter, Holborn, volume 1b, page 309. A death certificate could establish the exact date, place, recorded age, occupation, cause and informant, subject to the contents of the original record.
