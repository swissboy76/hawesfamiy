George and Beryl’s wedding

Identified by Antony Greenfield on 8 November 2026 as the wedding of George W. Hawes and Beryl. The wedding date, location and photographer have not yet been supplied.

Viewed upright, Beryl stands on the left wearing her wedding dress, veil and headpiece. George stands on the right in a suit with clerical collar and buttonhole flower. They hold hands.

Names and identification of the occasion come from family testimony. The original remains the historical source; the upright image differs only in orientation.
