Mary Emma Hawes

Biography

Mary Emma Hawes was born near her father's Strand practice and died aged 43 at the Stockwell family address.

Life and family history

Birth

The copied birth entry gives 7 November 1850 at 355 Strand, parish of St Clement Danes, London. Her parents were William Henry Bartleman Hawes, surgeon, and Sophia Maria Davison Hawes, formerly Day.

Death and brother's evidence

The family-held death abstract records Mary Emma's death on 8 December 1893 at 119 Bedford Road, Stockwell, aged 43. It lists influenza followed by phlebitis and exhaustion. Her brother W. E. Wilson Hawes was present and gave his address as 17 Sibella Road, Clapham. This is direct documentary evidence connecting the siblings.

Later family household

The Stockwell address was also the place where Sophia Maria died in February 1895, an important link between the family-held document and late-Victorian census research.
