The Hawes Family Archive · Biography

Richard Wesley Dowling Hawes

1848–1849

Richard died in infancy at the family home in Arlington Street. His birth and death were both recorded in a surviving handwritten family register.

Life and family history

Biography

Birth

A copied registration abstract records Richard's birth on 19 February 1848 at 13 Arlington Street, son of surgeon William Henry Bartleman Hawes and Sophia Maria Davison Hawes, formerly Day. Registration is dated 24 March 1848.

Death

The family compilation gives his death as 6 October 1849 at 13 Arlington Street, with inflammation of the bowels lasting four days as the cause. His father was present and served as the informant.

Discrepancy in the recorded age

The copied death entry gives Richard's age as 17 months, whereas the stated birth and death dates give about 19 months. Both readings are preserved until a certified register entry can resolve the discrepancy.
