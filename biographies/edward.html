William Edward Wilson Hawes

Name in family records: William Edward Wilson Hawes.

Childhood

A family-held handwritten birth-register abstract gives a birth on 22 June 1844 at 12 King William Street and notes the later registered name as William Edward Wilson. The family records give a baptism at St Martin-in-the-Fields on 17 August 1844; the original parish register has not yet been inspected. His father was involved in bankruptcy proceedings when William Edward was approximately ten.

Family and Surrey

The family records identify Mary Ellis as his wife and records seven children, including Walter Joseph William Hawes. Family testimony reports that Walter had eleven siblings, indicating twelve children altogether; this conflicts with the family records and remains to be reconciled. A South Norwood 1916 programme written and directed by Mrs Wilson Hawes (identified by the family as Mary Ellis) names several performers whom the family identifies as Walter’s siblings. The 1881 census reference RG11/820, folio 88, page 31 places the family in Croydon. The family associates his residence with Thornton Heath, but the precise address awaits a census transcription.

Working life

The family records describe William Edward as a bank clerk; this is now a recorded tree assertion, although the original census occupation column still needs checking. The census occupation field and later trade directories should settle the question.

Death and burial

A printed family memorial card records the death of William Edward Wilson-Hawes on 23 March 1900, aged 55, and burial at Tooting Cemetery. Its hyphenated surname offers a useful alternative spelling for searches. The family records also name Ashfield House, Feltham as his place of death; that location has not been independently verified.
