Marriage

Elizabeth Mullinex married William Hawes by banns at St George’s, Hanover Square, on 12 March 1805. Both were described as of the parish.

Household in 1841

The supplied transcript for 7 Adelphi Terrace lists Elizabeth Hawes, aged 55, with William and younger household members. Adult census ages were commonly rounded down; this is not an exact birth date.

Portrait attribution

The family suggests that the female portrait paired with William’s may depict Elizabeth rather than their daughter Maria. This remains unconfirmed and is not used as an identified tree portrait.
